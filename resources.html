Templates

What you fill in, what you receive

Only the first item is required for every event. Everything else either makes follow-up easier or comes back to you from SmartCT. Not all of these are public—the access column says who can get each one.

ItemWho uses itPurposeAccessStatus
Registration & attendance sheet Champions, Partners, Partner Coordinators Registrations, attendance, and the consent sign-off in the same form. Also asks whether the participant already has an ADA account and which email they used for it. It is how we know which Champion or Partner is responsible for which participants, and how completions get credited to your event. Issued on onboarding TBD
ADA email check Champions, Partners, Partner Coordinators A one-question survey run at the end of Part 1: which email did you use for your ADA account? Participants often sign up for the course with a different address than the one they gave you at registration, and that email is the only thing linking them to their completion. Issued on onboarding TBD
Post-event report Champions, Partners, Partner Coordinators Short: what happened, photos, and issues worth fixing. Not a second copy of the attendance list. Issued on onboarding TBD
Sanitized completion tracker Champions, Partners, Partner Coordinators You receive this, you don't fill it in. Completion status for your own participants, with personal data stripped—enough to see who still needs a nudge. Issued by the Program Officer TBD
Nudge message scripts Champions, Partner Coordinators Ready-made follow-up messages for self-paced chasing, English with Taglish variants. Issued on onboarding TBD
Social media caption pack Champions, Partners Co-branded captions and tagging guide for event posts. Issued on onboarding TBD
