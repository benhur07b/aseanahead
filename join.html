Event types

What event is for me?

Events come in six flavours—from The Soft Launch (a 30-minute intro that gets you registered and through Module 1) to The Hard Launch (the complete guided run, certificate included), with focused sessions like The Skill Drop, The Side Quest, The Glow-Up, and The Completion Sprint in between. Each one suits a different amount of time and a different goal—check the event titles in the schedule above against the type descriptions to pick yours.

Self-paced

Can't make any event? Go self-paced.

The self-paced track is open for the entire programme. Sign up whenever you're ready and complete the course at your own pace—ideally within 5 days, and within 10 days at the latest—with a facilitator backing you until you finish.

  1. Register for the self-paced track

    Use the registration link above—it's also listed in the schedule under Self-paced.

  2. Enroll in ASEAN Digital Academy

    Create your account on ada.aseanfoundation.org using an active email, then enroll in the AI Career Readiness Course.

  3. Work through the course at your own pace

    Follow the Participant Guide step by step—surveys and evaluations included. The course is mobile-friendly, so you can chip away at modules whenever you have time.

  4. Expect check-ins until you're done

    A champion will check in with you regularly—most actively during your first week. Aim to finish within 5 days of starting; the window closes at 10 days.

  5. Stuck? We're here to help

    Check out our Help Desk or contact your champion.

  6. Still stuck? Finish live instead

    You can re-join any weekly webinar and ride along from Part 2 as a guided finish—same course, same completion, extra support.
