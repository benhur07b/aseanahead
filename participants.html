Finish the course. Claim the perks.

Free course, an official certificate, 1-year LinkedIn Premium, plus opportunities such as Peer Mentorship and Virtual Job Fairs after completion: here's exactly how to get from sign-up to done.

What you get

Why complete the course

Credential

Official certificate

Issued on the ASEAN Digital Academy platform when you complete all requirements: a career credential you can put on your CV and LinkedIn.

Perk

1-year LinkedIn Premium

Granted after you provide—in the Post-Course Evaluation—your LinkedIn profile link and a CV improved using what you learned from the course. The ASEAN Foundation's AHEAD Team verifies both internally, then emails you how to activate your Premium.

Pathway

Mentorship & job fairs

Completing the course makes you eligible for the program's Peer Mentorship component and Virtual Job Fairs. Spots are limited and selection is competitive—see how it works.

Who it's for: university and TVET students and NEET youth across ASEAN. The course is self-paced, mobile-friendly, and open to youth looking to upskill.

Getting started

Your journey, step by step

  1. Create your ADA account

    Go to ada.aseanfoundation.org and register using an active email. This same email is used to verify your completion later.

  2. Enroll in the course

    Log in and open the AI Career Readiness Course.

  3. Take the pre-course survey and pre-course evaluation

    Do this before touching the modules.

    When filling in the evaluation, select:

    Philippines
    8. How did you hear about this programme?
    Smart Cities PH (SmartCT)

  4. Complete all five modules

    Each module has three video parts plus a PDF, with a pre-module test before and a post-module test after.

    Heads up on the Learning Material: the platform only marks it complete when you've gone through it to the very last page. If a module shows as unfinished, go back into its Learning Material and continue to the end—stopping partway through is the most common reason a module doesn't register as complete.

    Busy week? The course is built to flex around your schedule—you can move through the modules quickly now and revisit any video or Learning Material later. See Move at your own pace for how that works.

    Stuck? Having issues completing the modules? Check out our Help Desk or contact us.

  5. Join or start a discussion

    Reply to at least one discussion or create one in any module. The easiest way in: browse what others have posted and add to a thread that resonates with you. Make it count—share a genuine insight, question, or reflection from the module. Your posts build the learning community, and thoughtful contributions are what make it worth reading.

    Important: random or nonsensical text (e.g. "asdf", "done", a copy-pasted sentence with no connection to the module) may invalidate your completion and affect the benefits that come with it. One or two honest sentences about what you learned or want to ask is all it takes. And you're not limited to one—you can keep joining or starting discussions even after you've finished the modules.

  6. Finish the post-course evaluation

    Don't forget this step. Your completion doesn't count without it. Same as in the pre-course evaluation, select:
    7.Nationality
    Philippines
    8. How did you hear about this programme?
    Smart Cities PH (SmartCT)

    This is also where you claim your LinkedIn Premium: the evaluation asks for your LinkedIn profile link and a CV that follows what you learned from the course. Have both ready before you open it—leaving them out means the ASEAN Foundation can't verify your Premium grant.

  7. Get your certificate, then LinkedIn Premium

    Download your certificate from the platform. The 1-year LinkedIn Premium comes separately: the ASEAN Foundation—not SmartCT—verifies internally that the LinkedIn profile link you provided in the Post-Course Evaluation is valid and that your submitted CV follows what the course teaches. If everything checks out, the ASEAN Foundation AHEAD Team emails you instructions on how to activate your LinkedIn Premium. Expect results within 2–4 weeks; if something can't be verified, you may be asked to submit additional requirements.

  8. Congratulations! What's next?

    We may follow-up with a short post-course survey. Answering helps us match you with Virtual Job Fair opportunities and invite you to events near you. It's optional and never affects your certificate or LinkedIn Premium.

Don't miss these

The three completion requirements

The platform only counts you as complete—and unlocks your certificate—when all three are done:

  • Complete 100% of all modules in the course
  • Create at least one discussion, or participate in at least one discussion started by others, in any module
  • Complete the Post-Course Evaluation
Claiming the 1-year LinkedIn Premium takes two more things. The Post-Course Evaluation is also where you submit your LinkedIn profile link and a CV improved using what you learned from the course—there is no separate form. Have both ready before you open the evaluation: the ASEAN Foundation's AHEAD Team verifies both internally and, once everything is valid, emails you how to activate your Premium. Without them the grant can't be verified, and you may be asked to submit additional requirements later.
Make your discussion post meaningful. The discussion requirement isn't a box to tick—it's part of the learning. Joining an existing thread is a great way to start: read what others have shared and build on it. Posts that are random or nonsensical may invalidate your completion and what comes with it, so keep your contributions genuine. A short reflection or question about any module is enough—and the discussions stay open, so feel free to keep participating even after you've finished the modules.

Flexible by design

Move at your own pace—fast is fine

The course is intentionally low-friction. Videos count toward your progress as soon as you open them, and you can watch at whatever playback speed works for you. Learning Materials are marked complete once you've paged through to the end. None of this is an accident—the platform is built to trust you with your own learning, so a busy week, a slow connection, or content you already know never stands between you and finishing.

Completion is a floor, not a ceiling. Your access doesn't end when the progress bar hits 100%. You can return to any video, reread any Learning Material, and redo the exercises whenever you want—many completers come back to the modules when preparing their CV or getting ready for mentorship and job-fair selection. Move quickly now, go deeper later: both are valid ways to use the course.
Depth still pays off. The parts of the course that ask for genuine effort—the module tests, a meaningful discussion post, and the course exercises—are also the parts that count when the team selects participants for the mentorship component and Virtual Job Fairs. Finishing fast gets you the certificate; engaging deeply gets you considered for what comes next.

Why it works this way

Building an AI-Ready community

SmartCT's goal is to raise AI awareness and build an AI-Ready community in the Philippines and across the region. That goal shapes how we implement our projects: every barrier we remove means more young people start, finish, and join the community. So we deliberately keep the friction of joining and completing as low as we responsibly can, and we treat completion as the start of your membership in that community, not the end of your learning.

Opportunity scales with effort. Everyone who completes earns the certificate. The perks beyond it are earned in steps: LinkedIn Premium requires submitting real outputs (your LinkedIn profile and an improved CV), verified internally by the ASEAN Foundation, and the Peer Mentorship component and Virtual Job Fairs are limited and competitively selected—reserved for completers who put in the most effort. Wherever you are on that ladder, the modules stay open: you can always go back, watch the videos properly, and do the exercises when you're ready to climb.

After the course

Mentorship and job fairs—for select completers

Finishing the AI Career Readiness Course (which runs through December 2026) does more than earn your certificate and LinkedIn Premium—it makes you eligible for what comes next: the program's Peer Mentorship component and Virtual Job Fair events, where completers connect with mentors and meet potential employers.

Completing is how you're considered—but spots are limited. Only a select number of completers are invited into the mentorship component and job fairs, so completion makes you eligible rather than guaranteeing a place. Those in the running may be asked to submit a few additional requirements—for example, the outputs from your course exercises—so the team can select participants. Finishing the course, and finishing it well, is the best thing you can do to be considered. A practical tip: don't save it for December—completers who finish by November are in the best position when the mentorship and job-fair invitations go out.
After you complete, watch your inbox. We may follow-up with a short post-course survey. Answering helps us match you with Virtual Job Fair opportunities and invite you to events near you. It's optional and never affects your certificate or LinkedIn Premium.

Tips

Smooth sailing

On mobile? The course is mobile-friendly, but tests and discussions are easier on a laptop if you have access to one.

Low connectivity? Join a synchronous event (weekly webinars or an in-person session near you) where a champion guides the whole cohort through together.

Went async? If you joined through an intro event or the open call, your champion gives you dedicated support for your first 10 days—aim to finish while they're actively checking in, but the course stays open until the programme ends.

Stuck? Ask your champion first; they can solve most login, test, and playback issues on the spot.
