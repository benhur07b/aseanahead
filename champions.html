Guide · Champion

You are a motivator and guide—not an instructor.

The course teaches itself—your job is the completion, not the event. Whether you run sessions or support a self-paced cohort, you're accountable for getting every participant registered, moving, and across the finish line. This page is everything that takes, on both pathways.

Pathway · Support a cohort

Adopt learners who stalled—and earn when they finish

You don't need to run an event to land completions. Champions can opt in to support an assigned cohort of participants who started the course but haven't finished. Here's how it works, step by step:

  1. Opt in

    Tell the Program Officer you're ready to support a cohort. You need to be an onboarded Champion—course finished, orientation done.

  2. Get your assigned cohort

    SmartCT assigns you a batch of participants who registered and started but stalled along the way. You get a sanitized tracker for your cohort—enough to reach them and see where they stopped.

  3. Support them to the finish line

    Check in, answer questions, help with the common blockers (login, tests, the discussion post), and invite them to the next webinar if they'd rather finish live. The follow-up scripts and nudge templates are on the Resources page.

  4. Earn for every completion you help land
    Financial support for champions: PhP 3,000 for every 50 participants who successfully complete the course.

Synchronous

Complete everything live

In-person, webinar, or hybrid. The entire course is completed during the event—participants leave with all three completion requirements done. Plan at least two hours, run as two parts.

Pathway · Run events · Run of show

The standard synchronous agenda (Hard Launch event)

Run end to end, this agenda is The Hard Launch—the complete guided run. It runs in two parts. Part 1 takes everyone from registration through Module 1; run on its own, Part 1 is The Soft Launch, and participants who leave there finish the rest asynchronously. Part 2 continues live all the way to full completion of the course. Adjust the buffer, not the structure. Budget generously.

Pathway · Run events · Lifecycle

Before · During · After

  1. Before the event

    Choose the delivery mode. Pre-register participants. Check the venue and connectivity (or set up the online session). Prepare materials and confirm devices—the course is mobile-friendly, but laptops work better for tests and discussions.

  2. During the event

    Run the standard agenda. Capture the complete participant list with all required fields. Get every participant registered and enrolled on ADA before the event ends. Troubleshoot the common issues: login problems, test submission, video playback on mobile.

  3. After the event

    Submit your registration & attendance sheet and the short post-event report. SmartCT counter-checks your sheet against the ADA record and sends back a sanitized completion tracker—work from that to nudge weekly, and escalate persistent drop-offs to the Program Officer. The event isn't over until every participant has completed or the cohort window has closed.

Get started
